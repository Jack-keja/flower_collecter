# 🌸 Flower Collector

An engaging character-selection game built with Python and Pygame Zero! Choose your character, navigate the screen, and collect flowers to score points before time runs out!

## 🎮 Game Features

Flower Collector challenges players to choose between two charming characters – a duck or a wizard – and race against the clock to collect flowers scattered across the screen. Each flower collected earns points and resets the timer, giving you a chance to keep the streak going. How many flowers can you collect before time runs out?

## ✨ Features

- **Character Selection**: Choose between a duck or wizard character
- **Dynamic Flower Spawning**: Flowers appear at random positions after each collection
- **Timer-Based Gameplay**: Race against the clock with time resets on successful collection
- **Score Tracking**: Earn 10 points for each flower collected
- **Multiple Game States**: Smooth transitions between menu, gameplay, and game over screens
- **Boundary Detection**: Characters stay within screen limits
- **Intuitive Controls**: Simple arrow key movement system
- **Replay Functionality**: Return to menu and play again after game over

## 🎯 Who is this game for?

This game is designed for:
- Beginner to intermediate computer science students
- Students learning Python and Pygame Zero fundamentals
- Anyone interested in game state management concepts
- Developers learning character selection and sprite control systems

## 🛠️ What you need in advance

- **Python 3.6+**
- **Pygame Zero** (`pgzrun`)
- **Standard Libraries**: `time`, `random`

**🎮 How to Play**

- **Choose your character** - Click on either the duck or wizard on the menu screen
- **Navigate the screen** - Use arrow keys to move your character:
    - `Up Arrow: Move up`
    - `Down Arrow: Move down`
    - `Left Arrow: Move left`
    - `Right Arrow: Move right`
- **Collect flowers** - Move your character to touch the flower and score points
- **Beat the clock** - Each flower collected resets your timer, keep the chain going!
- **Game over** - When time runs out, your final score is displayed
- **Play again** - Press the left arrow key to return to the character selection menu
